# References

## Primary Papers

### 1. Foundation Models for Time Series: A Survey

**arXiv:** 2504.04011  
**Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.04011

This paper is used as the main survey/background paper. It explains the time-series foundation model landscape and organizes the field by architecture, input representation, prediction style, and training objective.

---

### 2. How Foundational are Foundation Models for Time Series Forecasting?

**arXiv:** 2510.00742  
**Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.00742

This paper is used as the main critical paper. It questions whether TSFMs are truly foundational and argues that zero-shot performance depends strongly on pretraining-domain alignment.

---

### 3. Rethinking Evaluation in the Era of Time Series Foundation Models: (Un)known Information Leakage Challenges

**arXiv:** 2510.13654  
**Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.13654

This paper is used for the evaluation section. It explains information leakage risks in TSFM benchmarking, including train-test sample overlap and temporal overlap of correlated series.

---

## Citation Note for Medium Article

This Medium article is an original review written in my own words. It is based on the three papers listed above. The article summarizes the main ideas, adds interpretation, and connects the papers into one short story about the promise, limits, and evaluation challenges of time-series foundation models.
